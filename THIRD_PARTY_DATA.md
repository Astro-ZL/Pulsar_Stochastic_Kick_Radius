# Observational data sources

`data/parallaxes_1.xlsx` compiles pulsar periods, proper motions, characteristic ages and parallaxes. Periods, proper motions and ages are drawn from the ATNF Pulsar Catalogue (Manchester et al. 2005). Parallaxes are compiled from timing, VLBI, Gaia DR2 and HST measurements, including the Cornell pulsar-parallax compilation.

- ATNF Pulsar Catalogue: https://www.atnf.csiro.au/research/pulsar/psrcat/
- Cornell parallax compilation: https://hosting.astro.cornell.edu/research/parallax/
- Bibliographic references: `docs/references.bib`.
- Source-level references: the `Reference` column in `data/catalog_selected_77.csv` and `data/catalog_selected_47.csv`.

Original observational measurements retain their source attribution and terms. CC BY 4.0 applies to author-generated derived products and documentation; it does not relicense third-party measurements.
